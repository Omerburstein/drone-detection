**Design decided 2026-08-18 — the order is reversed: ARD100-extra is primary, FL-Drones is a stretch.** M4a and M2b both showed that *uncontrolled variables dominate*: moving the IoU threshold 0.50→0.40 shifted `small_mav` precision 23 points, and target/background contrast alone swings small-target recall 19 points. Both are larger than the generalisation drop we are trying to measure, so M4b must **change exactly one thing — the video content — and hold everything else fixed.** ARD100-extra does that: identical 1080p, identical annotation standard and lab conventions, so every one of GLAD's hardcoded pixel constants stays valid and `prepare_ardmav` runs unmodified. It is also genuinely harder (ARD100 has the smallest average target of any published set, 0.01% of frame vs ARD-MAV's 0.02%, plus low light and abrupt camera movement), so a drop is informative. Its weakness — same lab, same campaign — is *bounded and statable*: the number is optimistic, and "GLAD retains X on unseen video from the same campaign" is still a true and useful sentence. FL-Drones' confounds are unbounded by comparison: three annotation lineages, and 752×480 against 1080p-tuned constants. **New blocker found for FL-Drones:** its targets run up to 259×197 px, while GLAD's motion branch caps blob area at 3000 px² (~55×55). Those targets are invisible to GMD/LMD *at any rescaling* — only the YOLO appearance branch sees them — and since the motion branches carry recall (0.51→0.81 in the ablation), that is a structural handicap masquerading as a generalisation failure. Rescale the constants by frame diagonal (752×480 → ×0.405 linear, ×0.164 for areas) **and** sweep the IoU threshold before believing any FL-Drones number.
